| 2026-11-24 | Mini-maps | Pre-rendered images (not live maps). | 2026-11-26 | Card variants | A second roll on top of rarity, so any card can come in a rarer version (like Pokémon reverse holos and CS2 StatTrak). Later: a StatTrak-style tracker (km travelled, days held or owners), foil pattern seeds with a few named rare patterns, special serials (#1, serials matching a fact about the place). Variant odds get published with the rarity odds; on the server the variant is rolled and stored in `openPack` | Developer agreed to all of them and chose Foil plus the Local stamp first. Gives the 70% of pulls that are Common a chance to be special |
| 2026-11-26 | Site theme: leather and gold | The whole site takes its colours from the card back and logo: near-black wine background with a burgundy glow, warm panels, cream text, gold for main buttons, active tabs and links, teal (burgundy's complement) for hometown and info. Rarity colours and card faces unchanged. Tokens in `web/src/index.css` | Developer: the site should be complementary to the logo and card back colours so it has one theme |
| 2026-11-26 | Card back and logo | Burgundy leather back with the gold wordmark and a compass pin (a map pin whose head holds a four-point compass star); the compass pin is the game's logo too. One back for everyone for now; collectible backs as rewards later | Developer picked it after four rounds of mock-ups (see [[Card Design Research#Card back (2026-11-26)]]): every card is a pin on the map, so the logo is one; leather stands out against the dark app |
| 2026-11-26 | Only Mythics are limited | Mythics stay 1 of 1 (46 in North America, no foil). Every other tier is unlimited: Rare, Epic and Legendary copies are numbered in pull order (foils counted separately), Commons unnumbered. Replaces the Legendary cap (/10, then /100 + foil /5) the same day | Developer: "I don't think the game would be feasible long term otherwise". Any capped tier eventually runs out and locks out new players; WikiGacha, a viral Wikipedia card gacha (2026), has no caps at all, so it never runs out. The 1/1 Mythics keep one truly scarce hook |
| 2026-11-26 | Legendary cap /100, foil /5 (superseded the same day) | Legendary goes from /10 + foil /5 to /100 + foil /5; the foil run is sized to the 5% foil odds so plain and foil sell out together | Developer, after the numbers: with 884 Legendaries in North America at /10, plain copies were gone in ~3 weeks at 1,000 players (~2 days at 10,000), and foils outlasted plains ~10x. At /100 + 5: ~7 months at 1,000 players, ~3 weeks at 10,000. Mythics (46, 1 each) stay as they are |
| 2026-11-26 | Caps only at the top (superseded the same day) | Only Legendary (/10, foil /5) and Mythic (1/1, no foil) are capped. Rare and Epic are unlimited but every copy is numbered in pull order ("Rare #4,812", foils counted separately); Commons unlimited and unnumbered. Replaces Rare /1000 and Epic /100 | Developer, after comparing with other TCGs: hard caps are normal in sports cards but rare in Pokémon, Lorcana and digital card games; capping everything would lock late players out of famous places within weeks of launch. Numbers without a cap keep low serials as bragging rights |
| 2026-11-26 | Local packs | One local pack a day, on top of the normal packs. Its 5 cards are near where you are: within 50 km, widening to 150 km and then your province or state when a rarity has nothing that close. Same rarity and foil odds as normal packs. Every card in it gets the Local sticker (place and date). In the demo, "where you are" is a town you pick (the hometown by default) | Developer's choices. 50 km keeps it truly local in cities; widening keeps rural packs from running out of Epics and Legendaries |

## Open
Each has the roadmap's recommendation.
- [ ] **Minimum age.** Recommended: 13+, with a birth-year check at sign-up. Collecting emails from children under 13 brings extra legal requirements.
- [ ] **Usernames.** Recommended: usernames are public on "discovered by" credits and leaderboards, so filter profanity at sign-up, add a report button, and allow renaming an account that breaks the rules.
- [ ] **Season policy.** (Developer, 2026-11-26: there are no seasons in the game; only relevant if a card refresh is ever planned.) Recommended: state publicly that Season 1 cards are never reprinted or taken away, except for botting. Later seasons add new variants instead.
- [ ] **Launch scope.** Recommended: the whole world at launch (more shareable), not Canada first. (Building the Phase 1 pipeline on Canada first is separate from launch scope.)
- [ ] **Local packs (idea, 2026-11-23; radius, frequency and odds decided 2026-11-26, see above).** Still to settle: v1 or v2; what "worth more" means for a Local-tagged copy (rarer variant, display only, or more points); how location is checked within the privacy rules (coarse IP region, never stored exactly) and how to stop VPN or GPS spoofing from faking travel. See [[Game Design#Sharing hooks]].
- [ ] **Analytics event list.** Write it before Phase 2: page view, sign-up, pack opened, card pulled (tier, serial), share tapped, friend added, map opened. These are the numbers the resume line depends on (see [[Resume & Metrics]]).

Drop Placedex (.com in use by another business) and Atlasdex (.com for sale at a premium). Confirm the handles first.

No Reddit threads from r/WebGames or r/incremental_games turned up for this kind of game.

**Takeaway:** CityDeck is close enough that the pitch has to lead with what it doesn't have: **"some places exist only once"** (serials and 1-of-1s), **the shared world map**, and **your hometown in pack one**. "Every city is a card" alone is no longer new. Worth playing CityDeck for 20 minutes before the paper prototype to see what it does well. This doesn't block Phase 0, but update the roadmap's competitor table.

# Card Design Research

Back to [[00 Home]]. Research for the card redesign (2026-11-26): how popular TCGs make their rare cards look and feel different from commons, and what Pull the Planet takes from that. See [[Game Design]] and [[Tasks]].

## What the big games do
- **Pokémon TCG (Scarlet & Violet, Mega Evolution):** commons are framed: a small art window, a big text box. Illustration Rares and Special Illustration Rares are full art: the illustration covers the whole card and shows the Pokémon in a scene (a place, a story), not just a portrait. Hyper Rares are gold, with a textured foil. Rarity is shown by symbols (dots, diamond, gold stars), and collectors often value the art-led SIRs above the technically rarer Hyper Rares.
- **Pokémon TCG Pocket (digital):** rarity as diamonds, stars and a crown. Three-star "Immersive" cards play a short animated scene when pulled and can be replayed; Crown rares are gold versions. The pack itself hints: special openings (light from the pack, a cracking sound) are guaranteed signals of a good pull.
- **Magic: The Gathering collector boosters:** the same card comes in treatments: borderless and extended art (the art runs past the frame), showcase frames themed to the set, foil, surge and textured foils, and serialized cards numbered like sports cards (under 1% of boosters). Prerelease and promo cards carry a gold stamp (a date or a symbol) that marks where and when they were obtained.
- **Disney Lorcana (Enchanted) and One Piece (alt art, "manga rare"):** the chase card is the normal card redrawn without a border: full-bleed art, heavy holo, a distinct rarity symbol. One Piece's top chase is a stylistic break (black-and-white manga panels).
- **Topps Chrome (sports cards):** a "rainbow" of parallels of one card, each a colour and each numbered smaller: refractor, then /199, /99, /50, /25, /10, /5, and a one-of-one Superfractor (a gold swirl pattern). The serial number is stamped on the card face.
- **Marvel Snap (digital):** upgrades are purely cosmetic and step up: the art breaks out of the frame (3D), then the frame becomes foil, prism, ink or gold, then animated particle "flares" are added.

## The shared philosophy
1. **Commons are framed, rares break the frame.** Higher tiers give more of the card to the art: art window, then extended art, then full bleed.
2. **The finish climbs a ladder.** Matte, then holo or foil, then textured or patterned foil, then gold or rainbow. On screen, foil means a shine that moves when the card tilts.
3. **The art tells a story at the top.** The most loved cards show the subject in its world, not just more shine.
4. **Scarcity is printed on the card.** A serial number stamped on the face, a one-of-one with its own look.
5. **The reveal is part of the card.** Anticipation before the flip, a different animation for the top tiers, replayable.
6. **Rarity reads at a glance** through symbols, not words.
7. **Stamps mark the story of a copy:** prerelease and promo stamps show where and when a card was pulled.

## Update 2026-11-26: no full art (developer)
The photos are Wikimedia Commons pictures of every shape and quality, not art made for cards, so full-bleed or extended art would look odd. The photo stays in a fixed window on every tier, and rarity goes into what we design ourselves: frame foil (like Pokémon's reverse holo: the frame shines, the picture stays clean), a richer frame per tier (like MTG showcase frames), map-themed foil patterns (contour lines, a latitude/longitude grid, a compass rose, rainbow for the Mythic), one photo treatment for all cards (bevel, vignette, light grade), printed details (coordinates, collector line, rarity symbols, serial stamp) and stamps (serial, Local, Hometown). The Epic and Legendary rows below are replaced by this.

## How this maps to Pull the Planet (first proposal, 2026-11-26)
- **Common:** classic framed card. Photo or mini-map in a window, a facts box (population, elevation and so on, like a TCG stats box), the flavour line in italics, a collector line (region code and number). Matte.
- **Rare:** same layout with a holo shine on the art window and frame; serial stamped.
- **Epic:** extended art: the photo runs to the card edges, and the text sits on a shaded strip. Foil frame.
- **Legendary:** full art with a gold, textured foil and a tilt shine; the serial on a gold stamp.
- **Mythic:** a one-of-one look (like a Superfractor): rainbow foil, an animated edge, a "1 of 1" seal, and its own reveal.
- **Rarity symbols** in the corner instead of the word (for example dot, diamond, two diamonds, star, crown).
- **Local stamp (developer's idea, 2026-11-26):** a card pulled from a local pack gets a sticker or stamp like a prerelease or passport stamp: "Local", the place it was pulled and the date. The same card from a random pack has no stamp, so the stamped copy is the special one.
- Problem to solve: Rare-and-up cards with no photo (18% of Rares, 7% of Epics, 4% of Legendaries) need a full-art look built from the mini-map.

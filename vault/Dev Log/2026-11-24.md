## Broke
- The first facts run was stopped by Claude Code when the computer ran low on memory, partway through the terrain-height step, which only saved at the end. It now saves after every batch of 100; the rerun finished in a few minutes.
- A heredoc edit with regex `\b` escapes failed its check again; regex edits go through the Edit tool.
- New towns rule, first tries: Wikidata alone kept Montreal's boroughs and dropped real villages; a bug searched for row numbers instead of names; "parish municipality" was read as a church parish. All fixed.
- QLever answered 429 Too Many Requests to the first all-of-North-America landmark query, then blocked even tiny queries for a few minutes. The step now sends smaller queries (one per nature class, one per country for buildings), pauses between them and waits a minute or more after a 429.
- A heredoc edit script turned `\n` escapes into real line breaks in `pageviews.py` again; fixed with the Edit tool. Edit scripts now go through a file written with the Write tool.
- pandas wrote `flavour_en.csv` with Windows line endings; changed back.
- The landmark queries took several tries. Asking the Wikidata query service for labels, articles and countries in the same query, or for many countries, wikis or classes at once, ran past its 60 s limit (answers cut off mid-stream). Now SPARQL only lists item IDs (one query per country, wiki and class for the US, Canada and Mexico) and the details come from the Wikidata API, 50 items a call. The API refused every call with `maxlag` (meant for editing bots); dropped. QLever kept throttling the per-country building queries, so the small countries go in one query.
- Natural Earth's borders are a few km off: Detroit's Renaissance Center and the American Falls came out Canadian. Fixed: when Wikidata's own country is within 10 km of the point, it wins.
- `facts.py` has its own `get` (web requests), which hid the country lookup of the same name; renamed the import.
- The Senate of Canada Building (former Union Station: "railway station") and Casino du Lac-Leamy ("heliport") were caught by the building exclusion words; `overrides.csv` keep rows now beat those words.
- The first full mini-map run stopped after 443 maps: an invalid Natural Earth lake shape broke clipping (GEOS "side location conflict"). Fixed with make_valid and a fallback; reruns skip maps already drawn.

- US run, towns step: one Wikidata query timed out (90 s) after about 5,000 of 7,301 neighbourhood checks and crashed the run. `wikidata_places.py` now retries timeouts and dropped connections, and saves its cache every 50 lookups instead of rewriting the whole file each time. The run resumed from the cache.

- US towns: the neighbourhood rule dropped about 520 real towns, because Wikidata calls them "borough of New Jersey" / "borough of Pennsylvania" / "New England town" (Fort Lee, West Mifflin, Westport) and any "borough" counted as a neighbourhood. Those labels now count as municipalities (New York City's and Montreal's boroughs still drop). US: 16,640 towns, 255 dropped (Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, East Boston...), 16,385 kept.

- US page views died at 03:00 (2026-11-25) on one dropped connection after ~27,500 of 52,399 articles. `pageviews.py` now retries dropped connections, and `run.py` reruns a failed step up to twice (2 min apart) since every step caches what it fetched; only a step that fails 3 times stops the run.

- US rarity crashed while printing "ī" (a Hawaiian name): on Windows, output sent to a log file is cp1252. `run.py` now runs every step with UTF-8 output.
- US gone check: 1,133 of 57,022 dropped (World Trade Center towers, Shea Stadium, Candlestick Park, the 1940 Tacoma Narrows Bridge, Kingda Ka); the current WTC and Tacoma bridges stay. 2 World Trade Center (only a foundation) came out Legendary; dropped in `overrides.csv`.
- US rarity, first pass: 55,889 cards: 30,811 Common, 20,063 Rare, 4,368 Epic, 646 Legendary, 1 Mythic (Rocky Mountains). Buildings 14,794, all Rare or better (developer's call).

- US facts step hung silently from 06:05 to 13:20 (2026-11-25) during terrain heights (Open-Meteo), after 14,100 of 31,048 points: most likely one server-requested wait with no limit. `facts.py` now caps waits at 5 min, logs each one, and retries dropped connections. Resumed from the cache.

- The 7-hour stall was Open-Meteo's free-tier quota (~600 points a minute, ~5,000 an hour, ~10,000 a day; the US needs 31,000 terrain heights). `facts.py` now stops asking when the hourly or daily allowance is used up, leaves the rest without a terrain height, and moves on; a rerun fills them in from the cache.
- A watcher now flags the US run if its log goes 20 minutes without a new line (developer asked, 2026-11-25).

- US run finished (2026-11-25 16:55): 55,888 cards in 51 regions (39.7 MB), 18,425 mini-maps; photo download retried once on a dropped connection and carried on.
- Duplicate cards: 37 US pairs and 4 Canadian ones shared a Wikidata item (Washington DC and Indianapolis as both town and building, planned towns and air bases Wikidata also calls structures, the Grand Canyon village linked to the canyon, Lexington twice). `gone.py` now keeps one card per item: a natural landmark beats a town (the town's link is wrong), a town beats a building, the bigger town beats the smaller.

- Building exclusion misfires found while picking Mythics: the Met, MoMA, National Gallery of Art, Brooklyn Museum ("nonprofit organization"), Hoover Dam ("power station") and Grand Central Terminal ("railway station") were out. Museums, galleries and dams now always kept; Grand Central and the National Gallery by hand. Built landmarks kept: 19,373 -> 19,861.

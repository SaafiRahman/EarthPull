## Done
- **Mythic globe dive** (pack opening step d, the last one): `web/src/Globe.tsx` (new) and `web/src/Reveal.tsx`. A Mythic's walkout now starts in space: the Earth spins in from the far side and turns until the place faces you (the country appears), dives (the region), tilts down over the spot (the kind of place), and a rainbow pin lands with a ring and a thump. The map dims, the card rises out of the pin with the rainbow rays, then the usual flash, flip, name and "One of one". About 7 s; a tap skips to the end.
  - MapLibre globe projection with OpenFreeMap tiles in the player's chosen map style; GSAP tweens a camera object so the dive stays in time with the sounds and lines.
  - How far it zooms comes from the card's area or length (a city frames the city, a building its block); with no fact, by type.
  - The country, region and kind lines get a dark halo so they read over a bright map.
  - No WebGL: the plain walkout plays. Legendary is unchanged (checked).
- Videos: `minimap-previews/special/mythic-globe-desktop.mp4` and `mythic-globe-phone.mp4` (Pointe-à-Pitre, Guadeloupe's Mythic). `record-special.mjs` waits longer for Mythics.

## Broke (and fixed)
- The globe was invisible at first: MapLibre's own CSS sets `position: relative` on the map's container, which beat Tailwind's `absolute inset-0`, so the map drew into a 400 x 300 canvas off to the side. The map now sits in its own inner element. (Global lesson `maplibre-container-css-wins`.)
- A tap during the dive closed the reveal instead of skipping: in development React mounts twice, and the first, thrown-away timeline was still started by its fallback timer and "finished" the reveal. The timer is now cancelled on cleanup. (Global lesson `strictmode-stale-timers-gsap`.)
- A stray dot showed in the middle of the screen before the pin landed (the ring's start state was drawn immediately); fixed.

## Later the same day: globe tour for every card
- Developer liked the dive and asked for it on every card: Commons and Rares zoom past fast, slower and more building the rarer the card, and a toggle if it's heavy.
- Built: **Effects button** in the header (Full / Light / Off, `effects.ts`). **Full:** one globe behind the whole pack screen (`TourMap.tsx`); as each card turns over the camera flies to its place and drops a pin in the card's colour (Common quick and high, Rare slower and lower, Epic flies to town level during its charge-up). Desktop's cascade now waits for the camera; on phones the globe follows the card you swipe to, but never to a face-down special card (it would give the place away). **Legendary** now gets the dive too, at 0.72x the Mythic's length, with a gold pin. **Light:** only the Legendary and Mythic dives. **Off:** no globe. See [[Decisions]].
- **Map data:** the first measurement was **21 MB for one pack**: OpenFreeMap's zoomed-out tiles are ~125 KB each, plus a shaded-relief image layer. Fixed by drawing the globe below zoom 7 from our own Natural Earth file (`web/public/world/world.json`, 0.55 MB compressed, cached after the first download; `web/scripts/build-world.py`). The first build was 6.5 MB because country and state outlines retraced every coastline; now only shared borders are kept. Result: Commons/Rares-only pack 0.06 MB, with an Epic up to ~2 MB (New York), with a Legendary dive ~1.2 MB.
- Broke: the first byte count missed most tiles, because MapLibre downloads them from web workers; `record-tour.mjs` now watches the workers too. A playthrough run once hit `ERR_CACHE_READ_FAILURE` loading the map worker (test browser cache); clean on rerun.
- Videos: `minimap-previews/tour/tour-epic-desktop.mp4`, `tour-epic-phone.mp4`, `tour-legendary-desktop.mp4`.

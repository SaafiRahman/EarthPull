Paper prototype: 30 cards

Real places, real data. Stats: HP = population (log scale), DEF = elevation, ATK = Wikipedia page views over 12 months (log scale), CHL = colder average temperature is higher. Each runs 1–100.

How to run it

  • Print pages 2–5 single-sided (100% scale, not "fit to page") and cut along the dashed lines. Page 6 holds card backs: print it on the back of each card sheet if your printer does duplex, or skip it and use opaque sleeves.
  • Shuffle all 30 face down. Each friend draws 5 as one "pack" and flips them one at a time.
  • Watch and write down: which cards get a reaction out loud, whether anyone reads the serial, whether anyone compares stats, and what they say about the Canadian towns.
